### Changed

- **A sentence stating a branch or an argument of a routine an entry lists is read against that routine's listing (T-580).** The agreement round holds a sentence of the body (section 5 and the passages written from the claims; not the closing sections, which are answered whole) that states a condition (a failure, a success, a zero or nonzero result, "if", "when", "only when", "unless", "otherwise") and a branch's outcome (the code returns, loops, exits, skips, rejects, aborts, stops, sleeps, waits, proceeds, continues, jumps, retries or falls through, or a branch is taken or reached; it calls or runs something only beside a condition word of its own: "if", "when", "whenever", "unless", "otherwise", "else", "only", "until", "while", "on success", "on the failure path"), an argument with such an outcome, or an argument by its place (`param_3`, "argument 2", "its first argument"), of a routine a ledger entry lists. It is held on every listed routine the clause of each outcome or argument names (with the antecedent of a relative "which"), or, for a clause naming none whose words point back ("With a path argument it rejects …"), the first routine the sentence before names; an address another clause names holds nothing. Each such routine is shown once as a `listing.N` fact with its code from the entry that lists it: whole within 1,200 characters, else its signature and the lines naming what the held sentences name (another address in any spelling the image bases make, a decompiler's prefix or a 32-bit name's zero padding allowed, a `param_N`, a function), with up to three lines around each, joined within the same budget with each gap counted and any line over 200 characters cut, else its head with the rest counted. The listings of one question show at most 24,000 characters, filled in passage order; a routine past that is not shown, no sentence is held on it alone, and the round says how many listings were left out, in the question and among what the report says it is missing. The sentence is asked under its own line: where the listing shows otherwise (the other branch, another argument or local), the model rewrites it on the listing, in its own words in section 5 when the row cites that listing, else it keeps it. Nothing is decided by code. The sentences and listings ride the existing single agreement question; no call is added, and a run with no such sentence is asked nothing more.
