### Fixed

- **The pack says what the sandbox's rows say of a value's process (T-739).** A behaviour link whose traffic the publish rule puts on a process the report does not list says so, in the rule's words with the process as the rows name it, and is never marked "process not attributed"; the links take the rule's decision, so a row of the sample's tree still decides, a value with a row naming no process reads as the rule reads it, and a URL or a request whose host is an address takes that address's row. The capture line states, per conversation, what the flow rows of the pack's own network view say of the remote address, read with the run's reader as the report's network block reads it: the processes they name, a flow of the sample's process tree, or that no flow row of the view holds the address. The words are computed where the pack line is written; the capture entry holds the tool's answer only. A view not read whole says nothing of any address, a value that does not parse as an address gets no words, and the words never cost a pack entry, a conversation or a cut the pack would not make without them: a bounded pack is laid out at the sizes its lines have without them, and each line shown whole takes them, in pack order, in the room left.
