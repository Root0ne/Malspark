### Fixed

- **A later stage is handed the open questions its upstream agents left, after every claim (T-878).** Under `inject_upstream: findings` the stage hand-off keeps its claims block first, byte for byte, and follows it with one block of the upstream agents' open questions, of every reason, each on one line as every summary writes it, behind the agent whose answer holds it (and the agent that left it open, where a callee's question was handed to its lead), under the heading `Open questions the upstream agents left (unknowns, not absences; each in its filer's words, one line, under the platform's field labels):`. The questions take only the room the claims leave under the block's limit (`core.reporting.upstream_findings_max_chars`), fitted line by line in order, never a line cut, with a last line counting those left out; there is no block where not even one line fits, and a claims block cut at the limit takes none. In the agent's input the questions stay only as far as every claim stays whole in the first chunk and a single chunk stays inside the agent's room; otherwise they are fitted again, line by line, to the chunk. `full`, which carries the upstream answers whole, gets no questions block, and a hand-off with no upstream question is unchanged.
