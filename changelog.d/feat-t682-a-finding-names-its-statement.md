### Added

- **A finding of a report round names the statement it is about.** A narrative or composer finding carries `statements`, value-free places in the checked answer (`key_findings.1`, `body#2`, `items.3.value`, `*` for the whole answer), in the replay record and in the run summary's unresolved rows. A finding's words and codes are unchanged, and a row stored without the field is read as before.
