### Fixed

- **One rule says where a sentence ends (T-719).** The section agreement round's sentences and the analyst report's reading of a flagged sentence follow one rule: a period ends no sentence where it closes a run of two or more single letters each followed by a period, or sits inside a number; every other period a space follows ends one. A closing quote or bracket after it is read past, and a closing mark of emphasis only where it closes emphasis opened earlier on its line and leaves none open after it, so a wildcard's `*.*` ends nothing. A table cell such as "names present, e.g. NAME_A and NAME_B; details not established" is one sentence, answered whole.
- **An answer copying a whole field answers each of its sentences (T-719).** A keep or a drop of the agreement round whose copied text is the whole of its passage is read as one row per sentence: each sentence ends exactly as the same answer given sentence by sentence would, with the same refusals and records. A rewrite copying more than one sentence is still not found: one text never stands in place of several sentences.
- **A finding first raised in a round's retry is recorded as not asked (T-719).** The agreement round and the question round record such a finding as the composer and the narrative do: not asked, saying that it first appeared in the answer to the round's one retry. In the agreement round a finding counts as asked where the sentence it is about was shown a finding of that code before, whatever rows carried it in either answer.
- **Section 11 states what left the machine (T-719).** The analyst report's limitations end on one line: for each kind of thing the run's disclosure record places outside the machine or cannot place, who received it and how many times, in each writer's short form (model requests, lookups with the kinds they were asked, sample uploads, tool calls, enrichment questions, tracing). Each receiver the record names is named, a tool server handed the sample too; one the record places outside with no answered call is stated as such; and where the record does not place a receiver, the line says so as the record has it: where it runs is not recorded, for a receiver whose host the record names, else destination not recorded. The enrichment questions name the hosts the record says they may reach, else say that their destination is not recorded. The line says that the full record is in the bundle, where the calls per role and every other detail stay. It holds hosts, kinds and counts only, and no model is shown it. A run whose record places nothing outside the machine prints no such line.
- **Three wordings (T-719).** A category the judge states as none takes no article; a dropped technique's row ends "(evidence: note)", and a row with no evidence prints no evidence bracket; and a trimmed count reads "n of N ledger entries was" or "were", agreeing with n.
