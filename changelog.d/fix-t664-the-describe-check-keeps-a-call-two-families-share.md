### Changed

- **The describe check keeps a call name that two technique families write (T-664).** A claim that names a call or program from a technique's card or rule describes that technique when at most two technique families (a parent and its sub-techniques) write the word; a word written by three families or more still names none of them. A call such as InternetReadFile, which a card and a rule of another family both name, now counts for the technique that lists it. No finding code, event or stored file changed.
