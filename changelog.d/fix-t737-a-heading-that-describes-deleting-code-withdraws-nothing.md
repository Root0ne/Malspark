### Fixed

- **A claim heading that describes deleting code withdraws nothing.** On a claim heading line
  of a validation retry, a word of withdrawing, removing, dropping or deleting is a withdrawal
  only in the clause that writes a claim number; a sentence end, a semicolon or a bracket ends
  a clause. Withdraw or retract in the heading's sentence, a withdrawing word in its note, a
  bare "Removed" opening it and the `WITHDRAW CLAIM` and `WITHDRAW FINDING` lines are read as
  before.
