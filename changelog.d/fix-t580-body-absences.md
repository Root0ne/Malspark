### Changed

- **A whole-code absence in any report section is answered against the body and the ledger, and a keep on no source reads the entry the sentence cites (T-580).** Beside every sentence of the conclusion and the limitations, the agreement round holds every whole-code absence of section 5 and of the passages written from the analysts' claims ("is therefore not shown to use …", "not confirmed", "no evidence of …"), and a keep of one is held as a closing keep is: on the words of a source that speaks of it (any passage but its own), or on the reason where no source, another passage of the body included, speaks of it. A body sentence is never left out: a keep a check still refuses after the retry stands, and every keep not tied to a source's words is recorded (`agreement.held_kept`). A source speaks of a body absence only where a run of one of its sentences between clause marks, short enough to print whole, passes the keep check as a quote, and the refusal names that run, so every refusal can be answered; an absence set in what a run saw (a run frame on its negated or first clause, an observing verb, or a run as its subject; never the code's "Run key" or "execution path") is spoken of only by a sentence stating what a run saw and denying nothing. A body keep resting only on other written passages, on words stating no absence, or in section 5 is recorded. A closing sentence or a held body absence that cites an entry whose rows record a value it names (a number that counts nothing and is no grouped number and not written as a date, or a quoted text) is shown those rows as a fact that speaks of it (`entry.N`), so "no source states it" is asked again with the rows. The rows state no absence: they back no closing keep, and the retry points to the rewrite on them; a closing sentence the model still keeps on a row quote after the retry is left out and recorded, true or not, as every closing keep the checks refuse is (T-568). A body absence kept on one of its rows, a value named as a whole word, is recorded. "Unknown" is read only as what is said of something, never as a noun's modifier. The absence reader takes what a negated "shown to <verb>" denies from the verb's object, and a value coordinated with the item before it ("processes 83 and 88") stays part of that item. A limitations sentence naming the later call by its kind ("read in a later disassembly") says the redo and is not held to the failed calls.
