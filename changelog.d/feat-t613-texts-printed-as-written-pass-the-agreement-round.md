### Changed

- **Every text the analyst report prints in its writer's own words is read by the agreement round (T-613).** The appendix's analyst statements, the ATT&CK table's claim sentence printed where no procedure stands, the judge's words in its note on a published technique and in its reason for one not published, and the judge's sentences section 7 prints are read off the analyst report's own render and shown to the round as passages marked `(verbatim)`, after every other passage. What decides is who wrote the text, never a section, a tool or a sample. Only the writer's own words are its passage: the platform's words around a judge's reason, its cut mark wherever it stands, and a note the platform writes, are none. One distinct text is one passage, naming every place it prints, and one sentence is one sentence wherever it prints, also inside two different texts, however often a text holds it and whatever the white space between its words: a sentence left out is left out at every place it prints, and a leaving-out wins over a keep of it elsewhere.
- **A verbatim passage is never rewritten (T-613).** A listed sentence of it is kept on a quote of a source that passes the keep checks, or dropped naming the source it contradicts: a fact, a section 5 passage, a debate round or a passage marked `(verbatim, a source only)`. A keep no check passes, a keep naming no source and a rewrite (refused, with the ends that pass) leave the sentence out, and so does a drop naming such a source that another check refuses. A drop naming no such source is refused and records the sentence as asked about and left standing (`agreement.listed`), as the text its place stores holds it: the analyst report treats it as any sentence a check left standing, the bundle marks it as asked about and left standing wherever that text prints (the ATT&CK table's status cell, the list of claims not published and the run's corroboration table among them, through the one marker every place uses), and a judge's words in that state stay printed. A sentence of a passage the round may rewrite keeps the words of its own mark. Where a verbatim sentence and a passage the round may rewrite disagree, the question says to list and mend the rewritable one.
- **A sentence left out is left out of the analyst report and recorded as left out (T-613).** In Markdown and in HTML it is left out where it would have printed and counted where the report counts what it leaves out: the appendix's own line for a statement, the section's line elsewhere; a claim sentence left out leaves the written procedure, or "-"; a judge's reason left out whole leaves the platform's words around it. The report's own fields, the bundle and the API keep the text whole. The flagged statement carries `left_out`, the places it is left out of: a state of its own, which the bundle, the API and the console state, and none lists as left standing. The console lists each of its lists under a heading saying what that list is. The round's record names the passage (`agreement.dropped`). The judge's verdict, classification, confidence, rating and decided techniques are never touched by the round.
- **What of a verbatim text's whole the analyst report does not print is a source of the round (T-613).** Where a text the round asks of is part of its writer's text (a claim's first sentence, a judge's reason cut to its width, the judge's sentences section 7 keeps beside a composed conclusion), the sentences the report prints nowhere are shown as a passage marked `(verbatim, a source only)`: named as a source, taking no row and no end of its own, and never the quote a keep stands on.
- **A sentence with no subject of its own is kept only on a quote naming its place (T-613).** A sentence such as "Not established.", "None." or one whose only content words are generic to the run is read against the label of the place it stands in: for a value cell, every word of the field's own name and what names its record (its index in its list, and its own name where no other record of the list shares it, so two channels differ; what the record is, for one standing alone); for a verbatim sentence, the technique it stands under, named by its whole id, or the judge's field it is. A place offering no label keeps no such sentence on a quote. The replay rebuilds each label, and who wrote each verbatim text, from the passage's identity in the stored report.
- **The run summary counts the verbatim texts the round was not shown (T-613).** The round's record (`section_agreement`) holds the verbatim texts it was shown (`verbatim_shown`, by passage), none where the question failed or was not asked, and, read off the finished report, how many it prints outside them (`verbatim_unseen`). Nothing printed changes. A render error while the texts are read, or while they are counted, is a recorded degradation reason.
- **The appendix counts a value as printed only where it prints (T-613).** A statement left out whole counts none of its values, so a later statement naming one is printed.
