### Changed

- **Every text the analyst report prints in its writer's own words is read by the agreement round (T-613).** The appendix's analyst statements, the ATT&CK table's claim sentence printed where no procedure stands, the judge's note on a published technique and its reason for one not published, and the judge's sentences section 7 prints are read off the analyst report's own render and shown to the round as passages marked `(verbatim)`, after every other passage. What decides is who wrote the text, never a section, a tool or a sample. Such a passage is never rewritten, since a rewrite would no longer be its writer's words: a listed sentence of it is kept on a quote of a source that passes the keep checks, or left out of the analyst report. A drop needs its reason; a keep on no passing quote, a keep naming no source and a rewrite (refused, with the ends that pass) are no keep. A sentence left out is left out of the analyst report, in Markdown and in HTML, where it would have printed, and counted where the report counts what it leaves out: the appendix's own line for a statement, the section's line elsewhere; a claim sentence left out leaves the written procedure, or "-". The report's own fields, the bundle and the API keep the text whole, and the round's record names the passage (`agreement.dropped`). The judge's verdict, classification, confidence, rating and decided techniques are never touched by the round. A run in which the round lists none of these sentences prints as before; only the question grows by the texts it shows.
- **A value cell's sentence with no subject of its own is kept only on a quote about its field (T-613).** A cell sentence such as "Not established." or "None." is read with the cell's own field label as its subject ("channel encryption", "encryption scheme library"), so a keep's quote holds two of the label's words and a negated sentence about another field backs none. A cell sentence with a subject of its own is read as before.
