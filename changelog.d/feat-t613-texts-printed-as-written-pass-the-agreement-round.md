### Changed

- **Every text the analyst report prints in its writer's own words is read by the agreement round (T-613).** The appendix's analyst statements, the ATT&CK table's claim sentence printed where no procedure stands, the judge's words in its note on a published technique and in its reason for one not published, and the judge's sentences section 7 prints are read off the analyst report's own render and shown to the round as passages marked `(verbatim)`, after every other passage. What decides is who wrote the text, never a section, a tool or a sample. Only the writer's own words are its passage: the platform's words around a judge's reason, and a note the platform writes, are none. One distinct text is one passage, naming every place it prints; a sentence of it left out is left out at each of them.
- **A verbatim passage is never rewritten (T-613).** A listed sentence of it is kept on a quote of a source that passes the keep checks, or dropped naming the source it contradicts (a fact, a section 5 passage, a debate round); a drop naming none is refused and leaves the sentence as written. Where a verbatim sentence and a passage the round may rewrite disagree, the question says to list and mend the rewritable one. A keep on no passing quote, a keep naming no source and a rewrite (refused, with the ends that pass) are no keep, and leave the sentence out.
- **A sentence left out is left out of the analyst report and recorded as left out (T-613).** In Markdown and in HTML it is left out where it would have printed and counted where the report counts what it leaves out: the appendix's own line for a statement, the section's line elsewhere; a claim sentence left out leaves the written procedure, or "-"; a judge's reason left out whole leaves the platform's words around it. The report's own fields, the bundle and the API keep the text whole. The flagged statement carries `left_out`, the places it is left out of: a state of its own, which the bundle, the API and the console state, and none lists as left standing. The round's record names the passage (`agreement.dropped`). The judge's verdict, classification, confidence, rating and decided techniques are never touched by the round.
- **The judge's words the analyst report does not print are a source of the round (T-613).** Beside a composed conclusion, the sentences of the judge's texts section 7 does not print are shown as a passage marked `(verbatim, a source only)`: named as a source, taking no row and no end of its own.
- **A sentence with no subject of its own is kept only on a quote naming its place (T-613).** A sentence such as "Not established." or "None." is read against the label of the place it stands in: for a value cell, every word of the field's own name and what names its record (its name or its index in its list, so two channels differ; what the record is, for one standing alone); for a verbatim sentence, the technique it stands under or the judge's field it is. A place offering no label keeps no such sentence on a quote. The replay rebuilds each label from the passage's identity in the stored report.
- **The run summary counts the verbatim texts the round was not shown (T-613).** The round's record (`section_agreement`) holds the verbatim texts it was shown (`verbatim_shown`, by passage) and, read off the finished report, how many it prints outside them (`verbatim_unseen`). Nothing printed changes. A render error while the texts are read is a recorded degradation reason.
- **The appendix counts a value as printed only where it prints (T-613).** A statement left out whole counts none of its values, so a later statement naming one is printed.
