### Fixed

- **A closing sentence kept on a quote of the body is not lost to a word test (T-705).** Where the agreement round keeps a sentence of the conclusion, the limitations, a value cell or a capability bullet on a quote that stands whole, in whole words, in one sentence of a section 5 passage it names, and the checks refuse the keep, after the retry, only on the words of what the sentence denies something of (the words before its negation, or what a denied item is), the sentence stands as written: the run records it under `agreement.kept_on_its_quote` with the source and the quote, and counts it (`kept_on_quote`) in the round's summary and log line. The source's sentence is not moved into another section, and no list of words decides what a sentence refers to. A quote standing in no source it names, or only in a fact or a debate round, one stating no absence, one of a passage written without the code, one no single sentence of its sources holds or that runs across two, and every keep refused for any other reason end as before; where the round itself leaves the source's sentence holding the quote out or changes it, the closing sentence is left out.
- **A refusal of a quote taken from a passage written without the code says so (T-705).** A keep of a closing sentence whose quote stands only in a named passage written without the code is told that the quote stands there ("only" there where no other passage it names holds it) and that such a passage backs no keep of the sentence, instead of being told that the quote stands in no source it names.
- **A sentence the agreement round leaves out or replaces is recorded whole (T-705).** Its row in `run_summary.validation.unresolved` carries the sentence whole under `stood`, however long; the message keeps its bounded copy.
