### Fixed

- **A closing sentence kept on a quote of the body is not lost to a word test (T-705).** Where the agreement round keeps a sentence of the conclusion, the limitations, a value cell or a capability bullet on a quote that stands word for word in a section 5 passage it names, and the checks refuse the keep, after the retry, only on the words of what the sentence denies something of (the words before its negation, or what a denied item is), the sentence is no longer left out: the one sentence of that passage holding the whole quote, in whole words, stands in its place, whole with its own citations, and the run records the replacement under `agreement.kept_in_source_words` with the sentence that stood, and counts it (`replaced`) in the round's summary and log line. Only a sentence that reads as prose on its own is moved: never a list item, a table row, a heading, a fragment opening as no sentence opens, or a sentence opening on a word that points to the sentences before it ("It", "Its", "They", "Their", "These", "This", "Those", "Such", "Which"), and never one the round itself leaves out or changes. The model's sentence itself is not kept, so the closing sections state only words the body states. A quote standing in no source it names, or only in a fact or a debate round, one stating no absence, one of a passage written without the code, one no single sentence of its sources holds or that runs across two, and every keep refused for any other reason end as before; a section 5 sentence the closing passage already prints is printed once, and the other sentence is left out.
- **A refusal of a quote taken from a passage written without the code says so (T-705).** A keep of a closing sentence whose quote stands only in a named passage written without the code is told that the quote stands there ("only" there where no other passage it names holds it) and that such a passage backs no keep of the sentence, instead of being told that the quote stands in no source it names.
- **A sentence the agreement round leaves out or replaces is recorded whole (T-705).** Its row in `run_summary.validation.unresolved` carries the sentence whole under `stood`, however long; the message keeps its bounded copy.
