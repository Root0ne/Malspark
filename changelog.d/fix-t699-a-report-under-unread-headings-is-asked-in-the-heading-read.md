### Fixed

- **An answer whose claims open under headings the claim reader does not read is asked in the heading it reads (T-699).** When a tool loop's last message writes claim field lines (`EVIDENCE:`, `CONFIDENCE:`, `TECHNIQUE:`) and no heading the claim grammar reads, the one final-answer nudge states the heading the platform reads, `CLAIM <n>: <sentence>`, from the same constants the grammar is tested against. It is the same single turn; a message with no field line gets the nudge's words as they were.
- **A numbered claim heading whose emphasis and note stand in either order before its delimiter is read (T-699).** `**CLAIM 1** (revises claim 2). …`, `**CLAIM 1 (revised).** …` and `CLAIM 1 (revised). …` open a claim with its number and its note, as `**CLAIM 1 (revised):** …` does: a numbered heading's note may follow the emphasis that closes its label, and a dot after a numbered heading's note is its delimiter. A dot after a heading with no note or no number, labels other than `CLAIM` (`C1`, `R1`), prose and lines under a DISPUTES section open no claim; every answer read before reads as it did, and a line still costs time linear in its length.
