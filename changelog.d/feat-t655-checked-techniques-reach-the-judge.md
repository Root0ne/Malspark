### Added

- **A technique an agent checked that nothing carries reaches the judge (T-655).** The judge's
  post-verdict technique question also lists every technique id an entry's call arguments
  write, whatever the tool, or a tool answer's declared technique field names, that no final
  claim, finding or bundle object carries, with where it was seen and the entries the passing
  agent's final claims cite. The judge maps it to the ledger entries it rests on or declines it
  with a reason, in the same call. A map is checked as an analyst's claim of the technique is
  (entries this run recorded, not only the lookups of the id, catalogue, absence, scope and
  describe checks, and no parent beside its published sub-technique); one that stands is
  published on its entries as a technique the judge states, one that fails is recorded as
  declined by the check's code and is not published. The question asks at most 32 checked
  techniques and only where the judge's window has room left after the others, names a few
  entries per agent, and records every id it leaves out as not asked, with why. A declined technique is recorded on the technique review and adds nothing to the
  printed report; a question with nothing checked reads as before.
  `scripts/replay/checked_techniques.py` rebuilds the list from a stored run.
