### Fixed

- **A closing `CONTRADICTIONS:` remark leaves the block right above it read (T-569).** A last label whose one line is a remark on the label line itself is no block of its own when nothing but blank lines stands between it and the block above, a line of that block holds the fields, and the remark writes no field, no claim reference and no ledger id and does not open with a wording of "none" ("nothing stands", "all of the above were resolved"); the block above is then read. Otherwise the last block is read as written, so a draft the mediator argued away stays argued away. A block ends at the next label.
- **A short name before "claim N" names the analyst holding the claim (T-569).** "STATIC claim 17" names the `static` analyst; on a line whose field leaves `static` out while its answer in force holds no claim 17, it names the one analyst of the field the form fits whose answer holds claim 17. A line of many references is read in time linear in its length.
