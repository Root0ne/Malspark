### Fixed

- **A closing `CONTRADICTIONS:` remark leaves the block right above it read (T-569).** A last label whose one line is a remark on the label line itself is no block of its own when nothing but blank lines stands between it and the block above, a line of that block holds the fields, the remark writes no field, no claim reference and no ledger id, it points at the block with a pointing word ("listed above", "preceding"), and the clause that holds the pointing word (a clause ends at ".", ";", "!" or "?", never at a comma) holds no word of an end, no negation and no zero ("is resolved", "no longer", "0 of the above remain"); the block above is then read. "listed above; the other differences do not hold up agreement" adopts it, "listed above, but none blocks" does not. Otherwise the last block is read as written, so a draft the mediator argued away stays argued away. A block ends at the next label.
- **A short name before "claim N" names the analyst holding the claim (T-569).** "STATIC claim 17" names the `static` analyst; on a line whose field leaves `static` out while its answer in force holds no claim 17, it names the one analyst of the field the form fits whose answer holds claim 17. A line of many references is read in time linear in its length.
