### Added

- **The score reader takes its verdict words from a key's declared scoring rules.** A table names the verdict words from worst to best, the lowest one the totals credit, the states that leave the denominator (under adjudication, does not hold for this file, unknown), the spellings that mean a verdict word and the annotations that may follow a verdict (a statement found only in the evidence bundle). `--rules` on the benchmark `score` and `gate` commands and on blind `prepare` and `agreement` reads key scores and blind sheets by it. A scored run reports its own denominator, the items that left it and, per annotation, the credited items that carry it; a word the table does not hold is refused, naming the row and the word. Without a table the reader is unchanged.
- **Agreement between scorers can be computed over a declared scale.** The ordinal order and the credited threshold come from the table, and a pair in which either scorer's cell is a state outside the denominator is left out of every statistic and counted per state.
