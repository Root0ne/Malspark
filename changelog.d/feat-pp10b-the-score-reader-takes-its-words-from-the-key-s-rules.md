### Added

- **The score reader takes its verdict words from a key's declared scoring rules.** A table names the verdict words from worst to best, the lowest one the totals credit, the states that leave the denominator (under adjudication, does not hold for this file, unknown), the spellings that mean a verdict word, the annotations that may follow a verdict and the verdicts each may follow (a statement found only in the evidence bundle), and the verdicts in the roles of missed and wrong. `--rules` on the benchmark `score` and `gate` commands and on blind `prepare` and `agreement` reads key scores and blind sheets by it. A scored run reports its own denominator, the items that left it and, per annotation, the credited items that carry it. A word the table does not hold, an annotation after a verdict it may not follow, and a repeated annotation are refused, naming the row and the word; a name is registered with one content only. The gate decides an item that some runs left out of their denominators over the runs that counted it, and counts it on its own line. Without a table the reader is unchanged.
- **Agreement between scorers can be computed over a declared scale.** The ordinal order and the credited threshold come from the table, a pair in which either scorer's cell is a state outside the denominator is left out of every statistic and counted per state, and leaving out `wrong` needs a verdict in that role.
