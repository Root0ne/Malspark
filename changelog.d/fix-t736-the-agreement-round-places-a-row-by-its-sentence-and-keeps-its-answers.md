### Fixed

- **The agreement round places a row by its sentence and keeps its answers in the run's record (T-736).** The round's retry names a passage by its id in square brackets, as the round's list writes it, and quotes each sentence it asks about whole; its record keeps a bounded copy. A row the round's schema accepts that names a passage the list does not write is placed by the sentences it copies where exactly one passage a row may answer holds them, each of them once; it is then checked and ended as any row naming that passage and recorded under `agreement.placed_by_sentence`. A copy with a sentence standing in two passages, in none, or twice in one leaves the row refused, so a copy of several sentences ends as the same sentences answered one by one. Every answer of the round, the first and the retry's, is kept as parsed in the round's record in the run summary (`section_agreement.answers`), masked as every stored model answer is, with whether the mask changed it (`section_agreement.answers_masked`); the answers are shown to no model, printed in no report and read by no value check. Re-applied through the round, a stored answer the mask left unchanged gives the round's ends exactly.
