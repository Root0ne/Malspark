### Fixed

- **A claim a blocking line named is answered by the revision, never carried beside it (T-696).** Where the analyst a blocking line of the round's mediation named revised, the one revision block that restates the named claim replaces it, whether or not it states every address of the claim; with no block restating it, the line rule withdraws it unless the revision's disputes defend it. Values no block states are still recorded as dropped. A claim no blocking line named keeps the subject test and stays beside a block that restates it without one of its addresses.
- **A dispute that rejects the analyst's own claim withdraws it (T-696).** "My own earlier claim 17 … I reject this", "My claims 3 and 4 are superseded by …", "I retract my earlier claim 2" and the like withdraw the claim of the answer in force they name, the dispute as the reason; a claim of another round's answer, a part of a claim ("my claim 3 confidence") and another analyst's claim rejected withdraw nothing. The wordings are declared in one place, `pipeline.retry_merge`.
