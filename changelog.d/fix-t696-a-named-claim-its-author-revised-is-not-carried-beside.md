### Fixed

- **No reading of a revision's free prose removes a claim by itself (T-696).** A claim in force leaves the merged answer only where a block of the revision replaces it, a `WITHDRAW` line or a block under its number that declares the withdrawal names it, or the line rule withdraws it; the line rule never withdraws a claim the revision's disputes refer to in any way, however the item reads. A range the disputes write backwards ("my claims 9-2 hold") refers to both its ends for the line rule and names no claim for any other purpose. A `WITHDRAW` line reads through the round note the claim list writes beside a claim, copied after the number (`WITHDRAW CLAIM 2 (unchanged since round 1): …`), by the one shape that writes it; any other bracket after the number makes the line a doubt.
- **A claim a blocking line speaks to is answered by the revision, never carried beside it (T-696).** Where a blocking line of the round's mediation speaks to a claim itself (the claim shares a ledger id or a value with the whole line, or the line writes four consecutive words of its sentence) and no own reference of the revision's disputes speaks for it, the revision block that restates it best (the largest share of its content words or values, the first on a tie) replaces it, whether or not it states every address of the claim, and the merge names that block for the claim (`restated_by`); values no block states are still recorded as dropped. Every other claim, named or not, keeps the address and subject tests and stays beside a block that restates it without one of its addresses; a claim a block restates is never withdrawn by rule.
- **A blocking line withdraws by rule only a claim it speaks to (T-696).** Where a claim the line names, of any analyst, shares a ledger id or a value (an address, a number) with the whole line, or has four consecutive words of its sentence written on it, the line speaks only to those claims; where none does, its rule reaches every claim it names. Other content words take no part. A claim it names without speaking to it is never withdrawn by rule by that line, keeps the line's mark, and the reference is stated.
- **What a prose withdrawal does and does not do (T-696).** A dispute item read as the author's withdrawal of its own claim ("My claim 4 is withdrawn", "My claim 2 (…). Withdrawn as stated", "I retract my earlier claim 2") only lets a block of the same revision that restates the claim replace it without the address test, where a blocking line speaks to that claim itself. It never withdraws the claim: with no restating block the claim stays in force, carried, with the line's mark. The reading is made for the safe side: a withdrawn word counts only with nothing but words of the passive between it and the reference and with the item's end, a sentence or clause mark, "as", "and", or "by" with the author or a claim after it; a verb on a pronoun in a later sentence counts only with no other subject (a reporting word, another's act, a mediation line, a role, a participant's name) and no keeping word between, and a plural pronoun never stands for one claim; "to" between two numbers is a range only when it rises and the second is no decimal. The wordings are declared in `pipeline.retry_merge`.
- **A revision that left a claim it began unread withdraws nothing by rule (T-696).** The revision node gives the merge the count the analyst's answer holds after its validation turn (a reason that claims it began were not read, a note that parts of the input were not analysed among them, and each block that stated no confidence), and the merge does not read the text again; a claim the line rule would withdraw is carried with its line, its mark kept, and the merge states why. The run summary says it in the platform's words: that the revision began claim blocks the platform did not read, that it states that parts of the input were not analysed, both joined, or otherwise that it records a reason its answer is not read whole, the answer's own words never quoted.
- **Reading which claims a mediation's lines name costs the lines' length (T-696).** A line's rows of a claim are kept once without searching the rows of earlier lines.
