### Fixed

- **A claim a blocking line named is answered by the revision, never carried beside it (T-696).** Where the analyst a blocking line of the round's mediation named revised, the revision block that restates the named claim best (the largest share of its content words or values, the first on a tie) replaces it, whether or not it states every address of the claim, and the merge names that block for the claim (`restated_by`), also where the block stands at another claim's place; with no block restating it, the line rule withdraws it unless the revision's disputes defend it. Values no block states are still recorded as dropped. A claim no blocking line named, or one the analyst's disputes speak for by a reference of its own that withdraws nothing, keeps the subject test and stays beside a block that restates it without one of its addresses.
- **A blocking line withdraws by rule only a claim it speaks to (T-696).** Where a claim the line names, of any analyst, shares a ledger id or a value (an address, a number) with the whole line, the line speaks only to the claims that share one, and to a claim whose sentence it quotes in four consecutive words; where none does, it speaks to every claim it names. Other content words take no part. A claim it names without speaking to it stands, for that line, as a claim no line named, with no mark from that line, and the reference is stated as not placed on it.
- **A dispute that unmistakably rejects the analyst's own claim withdraws it (T-696).** "My own earlier claim 17 … I reject this", "My claim 2 (…). Withdrawn as stated", "My claims 1 and 2 are superseded by …", "I retract my earlier claim 2" and the like withdraw the claim of the answer in force they name, read under the numbering of that answer, the dispute as the reason; a reference that states another round names no claim of it. A withdrawn word counts only before a mark or an end word, so "Rejected readings: …" withdraws nothing. An item reads as nothing where a word that affirms the claim stands right after the reference ("my claim 4 is correct"), a negation governs the withdrawing word (within three words before it, in its clause, outside brackets and quotations), a word of another's act has an agent other than the author after it ("rejected by the mediator"), a word of reported speech by anyone but the claim itself stands before a withdrawn word, a keeping or contrasting word follows the reference (except before the author's own verb on a pronoun in a later sentence), a conditional or a question stands in its sentences, the reference or the word is quoted, or the reference is a possessive or names a part of the claim; such a reference speaks for the claim. Another analyst's claim rejected withdraws nothing. The wordings are declared in one place, `pipeline.retry_merge`. A line that would count as a withdrawal not read exactly is not counted where it is a dispute item read as one.
- **A revision that left a claim it began unread withdraws nothing by rule (T-696).** Where the revision's reading holds a claim begun and not read, a claim a blocking line named is carried with that line, its mark kept, and the merge states why: the claim left unread may be the named claim restated.
