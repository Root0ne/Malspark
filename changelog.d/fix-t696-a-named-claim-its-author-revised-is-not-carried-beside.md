### Fixed

- **A claim a blocking line named is answered by the revision, never carried beside it (T-696).** Where the analyst a blocking line of the round's mediation named revised, the revision block that restates the named claim best (the largest share of its content words or values, the first on a tie) replaces it, whether or not it states every address of the claim, and the merge names that block for the claim (`restated_by`), also where the block stands at another claim's place; with no block restating it, the line rule withdraws it unless the revision's disputes defend it. Values no block states are still recorded as dropped. A claim no blocking line named, or one the analyst's disputes speak for by a reference of its own that withdraws nothing, keeps the subject test and stays beside a block that restates it without one of its addresses.
- **A blocking line withdraws by rule only a claim it speaks to (T-696).** A line speaks to a claim when the clause of a reference naming it shares a ledger id or an address with the claim, whatever the reference's form: a number, a list or a range. A claim it names without speaking to it stands as a claim no line named, with no mark from that line, and the reference is stated as not placed on it.
- **A dispute that unmistakably rejects the analyst's own claim withdraws it (T-696).** "My own earlier claim 17 … I reject this", "My claim 2 (…). Withdrawn as stated", "I retract my earlier claim 2" and the like withdraw the claim of the answer in force they name, the dispute as the reason, where the item shares a ledger id or an address with that claim or writes the round of the answer in force. An item reads as nothing where a negation stands between the reference and the withdrawing word, an agent follows the word ("rejected by the mediator"), a keeping or contrasting word follows the reference, a conditional or a question stands in its sentences, the reference or the word is quoted, or the reference is a possessive or names a part of the claim; such a reference speaks for the claim. A claim of another round's answer and another analyst's claim rejected withdraw nothing. The wordings are declared in one place, `pipeline.retry_merge`. A line counted as a withdrawal not read exactly is no longer counted where it is a dispute item read as one.
