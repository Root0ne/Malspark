### Changed

- **The agreement question names the call that redid a failed call (T-600).** Its facts on a failed call and the later call that answered for the same address name, for a call a later call redid, the call the run summary names as the redo (the same question asked again). The run summary, the limitations facts and the agreement question read one redo map.
- **A denial of a name the run holds is read against the ledger (T-600).** A sentence of any passage the agreement question may rewrite, the conclusion and the limitations included, that denies something of an API, an export or a decoded or plain text the run's answers hold is held, and the question is shown what the ledger holds of that name: the routines the function index lists calling the API and through which slot, with the call lines of each listed routine and every slot they write named; the export's names, address, callees and their callees, and its listing; the routines a decoded or plain text is tied to. An API or an export spelled like a word of prose counts only written as code or beside a word saying it is a function; an export or an import counts only from an answer about the sample itself. A sentence held this way is held on every name it writes. A negated sentence naming no such name and no address that opens by pointing back ("These names are …", "It is not …") is held on the names of the sentence before it, where its denial is of what it points back to: a negated clause writes the word pointing back, or denies something of no subject of its own ("They serve the lookup, not a shadow-copy routine"); one that denies something of its own ("It resolves the hashes, so the working API set is not in the import table") is not held. A denial set in what a run saw is not read this way. A name written as a call ("`connect()`") is read as the name. A sentence is read without its citations, wherever they stand: an entry id cuts no clause and names nothing, and one written between two words leaves them two words. Code decides none of these sentences from their words: the model answers each one with the fact shown. A closing sentence held this way is not kept as written, and the same words with a citation added are a keep: the question and every refusal say so, and say what it may do instead, a rewrite in the model's own words on the lines shown, citing the entry they come from and saying what the lines show and what stays unknown, or a drop. A body sentence held this way is kept naming the fact and quoting the words of one of its rows that name what the sentence names, rewritten on the lines shown, citing an entry the fact cites, or dropped: the question and every refusal say so; a refusal of its rewrite offers that keep, never a keep as written. A fact of a name speaks only to the sentences it is shown for. The facts of names fill at most 24,000 characters per question, the conclusion's and the limitations' first, then the value cells' and capability bullets', then the rest; the rest are counted.
- **A limitations sentence naming a redone call is held to the failed calls (T-600).** A negated sentence that names a failed call a later call redid by an address the call asked for together with its tool or kind, or cites the failed call, and cites none of the calls that redid it, is held as one saying the call failed is, whatever else it says. The question names, for each such sentence, the calls that redid it. A keep of it is refused, and the same words with a citation added are a keep; the question and the refusal name the same entries and say what it may do: a rewrite in the model's own words on what those calls answered, citing them, or a drop. A rewrite of it, or of a limitations sentence saying a redone call failed, cites one of the calls that redid each call the sentence is held to, read by its entry ids alone, whatever its words; else it is told what a keep of the sentence is told. A limitations sentence saying a call failed that a later call redid is told, when kept, to be rewritten citing the calls that redid it or dropped; a rewrite of the conclusion adding such a failure is told the same, and a rewrite of a body passage is told to cite the calls that redid them, in their number, or keep the sentence as a keep of it passes. Every refusal of a held closing sentence offers a drop where it would otherwise offer a keep, and names its calls whole, in their number; a rewrite of a closing sentence no gate holds that adds such a failure is offered the keep that passes for it. The same words count as the same whatever citations they carry and wherever those stand.
- **A held sentence never prints unless an answer for it passes (T-600).** A sentence held to the ledger's own lines (one denying something of a name the run holds, a limitations sentence naming a failed call a later call redid, or one saying such a call failed) that no answer passing the checks answers after the retry is left out of the report, of the body as of the conclusion and the limitations, and recorded as a sentence left out with what holds it: one whose last answer a check refused with every code that refused it, one no answer listed with that reason, and, where the question got no answer at all (its call failed, the spend ceiling stopped it, or no row of the answer could be read), each such sentence with that one reason; every other sentence then stands as written. One the passage writes more than once, which no row can name, is left out each time it stands, with that reason; no refusal is raised for it, so it opens no retry. An answer that passes answers its sentence, whichever row comes first: a refused row for the same sentence is not applied, leaves nothing out, records nothing and asks nothing more, so it opens no retry, in every passage, the conclusion and the limitations included, where a refused keep beside a passing one used to leave the sentence out, and a section 5 sentence answered by a passing rewrite and a refused one takes the rewrite whichever row comes first, where the platform's qualification used to take it when the refused row came first. A refused answer from the first turn that the retry does not answer again stays that sentence's last answer. Where the platform already changes such a sentence (the fact's own clause added to a section 5 sentence on a decoded value) or already leaves it out of a closing section, that end stands with its own record; one that also calls code an entry lists not read is left out, as its question says, never qualified with the ledger's listing fact. A drop of such a sentence is an answer in the body too, recorded as dropped by the round with what holds it. A rewrite of a body sentence held this way in the same words, a citation added wherever it stands, is the keep it is: it stands only quoting a row of the fact shown for it. A rewrite of such a sentence follows each clause of it that denies something with a citation, at once, of an entry shown for that sentence (in the limitations, also a failed call its facts show that no later call redid); a denial whose citation stands only in another clause, or follows with an entry not shown for it, is refused, in one finding naming the first three such denials and counting the rest. The question and every refusal of an answer for such a sentence say all of this; the run summary and the console count every sentence left out. A key finding whose only sentence is left out is not printed as an empty item, and an execution step whose only sentence is left out reads "-". A printed line saying nothing was written for a passage (a capability subsection, the background, the summary) says instead how many sentences the report model wrote there were left out. Every answer for any other sentence ends as before.
- **A value cell and a capability bullet are answered whole (T-600).** Each value cell of a record table (a channel's protocol, encryption and packet layout; an encryption scheme's cipher, mode, library and IV) and each capability bullet of the analyst report's summary is a passage the agreement question may rewrite, and every sentence of it is answered, as every sentence of the conclusion and the limitations is: held for being that kind of field, never for its words. A keep on a source's words, a keep on the reason where no source speaks of it, a rewrite on a source or a drop with its reason stands; where no answer for a sentence of it passes the checks after the retry, refused or never given, the sentence is left out and recorded, a cell left with no sentence printing "-" and a bullet left with none not printed. Where the question got no answer at all, a cell or bullet nothing but its kind holds prints as written; one the ledger's own lines hold is left out as any such sentence is. The question says once, for all such fields, what becomes of a sentence no answer passes and what an emptied cell or bullet prints, and every request for a sentence no row listed, and the last refusal of each row answering one, says once what the field is, the ends that pass and that end; where no row of the answer could be read, nothing is asked of a cell or bullet nothing but its kind holds. A sentence of such a field calling code an entry lists not read is left out where a row of the answer was read, as every such sentence no answer passes is, never qualified with the ledger's listing fact. A bullet is a passage of its own, tied to no key finding. The question no longer says a table cell is listed with keep and never rewritten: only a passage marked read only (a row of the configuration, command or flag tables) is. In the analyst report, a procedure the agreement question left out prints "-" in the ATT&CK table, never an analyst's claim in its place; in the bundle, a host identifier's purpose it left out says so, and an encryption-scheme field it left out prints "-" in its row. The bundle's line saying no summary is printed counts only the summary and key findings it prints, never the bullets, which only the analyst report prints.
- **Every refusal offers only the ends that pass (T-600).** A closing sentence, a value cell's or a capability bullet's, that the ledger says otherwise of whatever a keep of it names (a failed call a later call redid, code an entry lists called not read, a count of failed calls the ledger's count does not hold), and a body sentence held on a name that calls such code not read or gives such a count, is offered a rewrite or a drop alone: every refusal and every request for it says a keep of it is refused whatever it names, and the same words with a citation added are a keep, refused the same way. A refusal of a closing keep saying a redone call failed says so of the sentence, not of a rewrite, and offers a rewrite citing the calls that redid it or a drop. A refusal of a sentence calling code an entry lists not read offers a drop wherever one passes and says what becomes of the sentence where that answer is its last: in the body, the platform appends the ledger's listing fact; in the conclusion or the limitations, a keep, or a rewrite resting on the sources it names, leaves it out, and a rewrite naming none, a refused drop, or no row, leaves it with the fact appended; a rewrite still calling it so is named as the rewrite, and one calling code not read where its sentence does not is told no end of the sentence. A row answering a closing sentence whose own refusals name the listing is told that end by them; one refused for anything else is asked about the listing too, told the end its refusal brings. A drop of a body sentence nothing holds is refused saying which sentences may be left out; a drop of the body naming no sentence of its passage is refused for that alone, asked for no source. A keep naming no source of a sentence no keep passes for is asked only for a drop's reason, never for sources. The last refusal of a row answering a sentence the ledger's lines hold says the ends that pass for it, once, where no refusal of the row says them already. The question lists each sentence of the conclusion, the limitations, a value cell or a capability bullet that the ledger says otherwise of whatever a keep of it names under the ends that pass for it, and a body sentence held on a name that no keep passes for under a rewrite or a drop alone. One table test walks every refusal the round can raise, for every kind of passage it answers and every answer that raises it, asserting each whole and checking what the round does; over every kind and every answer it reads the ends each refusal offers off the refusal by the round's own end texts, feeds each back and finds it passes, and finds every end that passes offered, save in the refusals of a plain sentence of the conclusion or the limitations, which it lists by code; the question's lines on each kind are asserted whole the same way.
- **A citation inside a code span leaves the name whole (T-600).** A sentence is read without its citations, and one written inside a code span ("`Query[ev_0109]HostMode`") leaves the name as one word.
- **A quote shorter than the floor is refused as such (T-600).** The agreement question states once that a quote is at least 12 characters of the source's words, copied exactly, and a keep whose quote is shorter is refused for that, told to quote at least that many characters where a keep of the sentence can pass, rather than told its quote does not stand in a source it names; a keep naming a source with no quote at all is told it gives no quote.
