### Changed

- **The agreement question names the call that redid a failed call (T-600).** Its facts on a failed call and the later call that answered for the same address name, for a call a later call redid, the call the run summary names as the redo (the same question asked again). The run summary, the limitations facts and the agreement question read one redo map.
- **A denial of a name the run holds is read against the ledger (T-600).** A sentence of any passage the agreement question may rewrite, the conclusion and the limitations included, that denies something of an API, an export or a decoded or plain text the run's answers hold is held, and the question is shown what the ledger holds of that name: the routines the function index lists calling the API and through which slot, with the call lines of each listed routine and every slot they write named; the export's names, address, callees and their callees, and its listing; the routines a decoded or plain text is tied to. An API or an export spelled like a word of prose counts only written as code or beside a word saying it is a function; an export or an import counts only from an answer about the sample itself. A sentence held this way is held on every name it writes. A negated sentence naming no such name and no address that opens by pointing back ("These names are …", "It is not …") is held on the names of the sentence before it. A denial set in what a run saw is not read this way. A name written as a call ("`connect()`") is read as the name. A sentence is read without its citations, wherever they stand: an entry id cuts no clause and names nothing, and one written between two words leaves them two words. Code decides none of these sentences from their words: the model answers each one with the fact shown. A closing sentence held this way is not kept as written, and the same words with a citation added are a keep: the question and every refusal say so, and say what it may do instead, a rewrite in the model's own words on the lines shown, citing the entry they come from and saying what the lines show and what stays unknown, or a drop. A body sentence held this way is kept naming the fact and quoting the words of one of its rows that name what the sentence names, or rewritten on the lines shown, citing an entry the fact cites: the question and every refusal say so; a refusal of its rewrite offers that keep, never a keep as written. A fact of a name speaks only to the sentences it is shown for. The facts of names fill at most 24,000 characters per question; the rest are counted.
- **A limitations sentence naming a redone call is held to the failed calls (T-600).** A negated sentence that names a failed call a later call redid by an address the call asked for together with its tool or kind, or cites the failed call, and cites none of the calls that redid it, is held as one saying the call failed is, whatever else it says. The question names, for each such sentence, the calls that redid it. A keep of it is refused, and the same words with a citation added are a keep; the question and the refusal name the same entries and say what it may do: a rewrite in the model's own words on what those calls answered, citing them, or a drop. A rewrite of it, or of a limitations sentence saying a redone call failed, cites one of the calls that redid each call the sentence is held to, read by its entry ids alone, whatever its words; else it is told what a keep of the sentence is told. A limitations sentence saying a call failed that a later call redid is told, when kept, to be rewritten citing the calls that redid it or dropped; a rewrite of the conclusion adding such a failure is told the same, and a rewrite of a body passage is told to cite the calls that redid them, in their number, or keep the sentence as a keep of it passes. Every refusal of a held closing sentence offers a drop where it would otherwise offer a keep, and names its calls whole, in their number; a rewrite of a closing sentence no gate holds that adds such a failure is offered the keep that passes for it. The same words count as the same whatever citations they carry and wherever those stand.
- **A held sentence never prints as it was refused (T-600).** A sentence held to the ledger's own lines (one denying something of a name the run holds, a limitations sentence naming a failed call a later call redid, or one saying such a call failed) whose last answer after the retry a check still refuses is left out of the report, of the body as of the conclusion and the limitations, and recorded as a sentence left out, with what holds it and every code that refused the answer; the run summary and the console count it. The question and every refusal of an answer for such a sentence say so. A key finding whose only sentence is left out is not printed as an empty item, and an execution step whose only sentence is left out reads "-". Every other refused answer ends as before.
- **A citation inside a code span leaves the name whole (T-600).** A sentence is read without its citations, and one written inside a code span ("`Query[ev_0109]HostMode`") leaves the name as one word.
