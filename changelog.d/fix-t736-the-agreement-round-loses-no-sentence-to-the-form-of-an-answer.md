### Fixed

- **The agreement round loses no sentence to the form of an answer (T-736).** The round's retry names a passage only by its id in square brackets, as the round's list writes it, and quotes each sentence it asks about whole, its record keeping a bounded copy; no token in the shape of an id the list does not write is shown. A row naming a passage the list does not write is placed by the sentence it copies, where that sentence stands whole in exactly one passage a row may answer, then checked as any row and recorded under `agreement.placed_by_sentence`; a sentence standing in two passages or in none leaves the row refused. A closing sentence, a value cell or a capability bullet kept on a quote that stands whole in one sentence of a section 5 passage, and refused only by the test of the words the sentence denies something of, stands as written and is recorded under `agreement.kept_on_its_quote` (counted `kept_on_quote`); the source's sentence is not moved into another section and no word list decides what a sentence refers to (`agreement.kept_in_source_words` and the `replaced` count are gone). A section 5 rewrite resting on the fact of a routine whose code no entry lists stands in the report model's words, either way, checked as any rewrite, with no platform clause appended; every other fact's clause is unchanged. Every answer of the round, the first and the retry's, is kept as parsed in the round's record in the run summary (`section_agreement.answers`), shown to no model and printed in no report, so a stored run is re-applied through the round with no model call.
