### Changed

- **A debate's first mediation asks for every contradiction now (T-700).** Its question carries one sentence before the closing line: list every contradiction the answers and the ledger establish now, because a point first raised in a later mediation costs the debate a revision round. A later mediation is asked as before. The sentence asks; what the mediator lists and how it marks each line stay its own decision.
- **A later mediation is told the round each claim's text was last written (T-700).** From a debate's second mediation on, each claim whose text an earlier mediation read states on its line "unchanged since round N" (a claim a merge carried, or one kept beside a validation retry's additions) or "written in round N" (a claim of an answer of an earlier round); a claim written in the last revision round carries no note. The carried-or-new question asks, for a line marked [new] on such a claim, what is new on it (an entry, an answer of another analyst). It is a question: no line is dropped or re-marked on it, and every other surface renders the claims as before.
