### Changed

- **A bracketed operand in a sentence is not read as a citation (T-656).** A citation lists ids, words or numbered references; a memory operand written in prose, such as `GS:[0x60]` or `[rax+rcx*4+0x10]`, was read as the citation `[0x60]` and the section was asked again. A bracketed group whose whole content is numerals and bare names joined by `+`, `-` or `*`, holding a `0x` numeral or a sum or a product, is now notation the citation check does not read, alone or beside real citations. Every form a writer cites with is still asked about with the same sentence as before: an unissued or mis-padded id, a heading, a numbered reference or range (`[12]`, `[1-3]`), a range of ids, a name with digits in it (`[E12]`, `[ebp+var_4]`) and the evidence prefix written as a name (`[ev+12]`). The check runs in time linear in the text.
