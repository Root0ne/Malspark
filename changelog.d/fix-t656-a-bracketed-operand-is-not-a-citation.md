### Changed

- **A bracketed operand in a sentence is not read as a citation (T-656).** A citation lists ids, words or numbered references; a memory operand written in prose, such as `GS:[0x60]` or `[rax+rcx*4+0x10]`, was read as the citation `[0x60]` and the section was asked again. A bracketed group is now an operand, which the citation check does not read alone or beside real citations, when its whole content is ASCII numerals and bare names joined by `+`, `-` or `*` and it holds a `0x` numeral, or a `+` or `*` with both a name and a numeral among its terms. Everything else is still asked about with the same sentence as before: an unissued or mis-padded id, a heading, a numbered reference, range or sum (`[12]`, `[1-3]`, `[12+13]`), names alone (`[capa+FLOSS]`), a name minus a decimal (`[rbp-8]`), a range of ids, a name with digits in it (`[E12]`, `[ebp+var_4]`), and any group that writes the evidence prefix or the word evidence as a name (`[ev_+12]`, `[evidence+12]`). The check runs in time linear in the text.
