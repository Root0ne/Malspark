### Changed

- **A claim that does not describe its technique opens no analyst turn of its own (T-657).** The finding `attck.claim_does_not_describe` is still raised on every such claim. Beside a finding that opens the validation turn it is asked there exactly as before, with the technique's card. Standing alone (beside nothing but a check that could not run, or the exit branches that joined it) no turn is asked and no model call is made: the finding is recorded as not asked with a sentence saying why, published on the live conversation as a finding nobody was shown, the claims stand as written, and the judge's technique question asks about each technique no claim naming it describes, as it already did for a technique the analyst kept when asked. Every other finding opens its turn as before. `python -m scripts.replay.retry_turns <run dir>` lists a stored run's analyst retry episodes and the ones this rule would not open, with their calls and priced cost.
